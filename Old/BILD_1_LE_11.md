### What happens to the oxygen gas you inhale?

Equation: C6H12O6 + O2 → CO2 + H2O

A. It becomes part of carbon dioxide molecules.
B. It becomes part of water molecules.
C. It is exhaled unchanged.
D. A and B
E. B and C

#### Answer Explanation:
- A. It becomes part of carbon dioxide molecules
  - No: CO2 comes from the sugar in transition reaction and citric acid cycle
- B. It becomes part of water molecules
  - Yes: in electron transport chain
- C. It is exhaled unchanged
  - Yes: this is why mouth-to-mouth resuscitation works

### Photosynthesis vs. cellular respiration

Photosynthesis: Uses sunlight energy to turn CO2 and H2O into O2 and glucose
- CO2 + H2O → Glucose + O2
- Light energy

Cellular Respiration: Breaks down glucose using O2 to get CO2, H2O and chemical energy in ATP
- Glucose + O2 → CO2 + H2O
- Pi + ADP → ATP

### A note about the diversity of organisms in biology...

Every living organism needs from its food:
- Carbon and other atoms
- Energy

Not all of them get carbon from sugars or CO2 and energy from glucose using oxygen. Examples:

- Bacteria in acid hot springs:
  - Carbon from CO2
  - Energy from Fe2S using O2

- Some archaebacteria that process sulfur:
  - Carbon from vinegar
  - Energy from sulfate
