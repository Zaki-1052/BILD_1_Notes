## Big Idea: Cellular Respiration
- Cellular respiration transforms energy from the chemical bonds of food into energy in the chemical bonds of ATP.
### Chemical Equation
Glucose + 6O₂ → 6CO₂ + 6H₂O

#### Mass and Energy Transformation
- Mass transformed into mass:
	- Glucose & O₂ to CO₂ & H₂O
- Energy transformed into energy:
	- Bonds of Glucose to Bonds of ATP
	- Pi + ADP → ATP

## Four Stages of Cellular Respiration
### Get That Cell Energy!

C₆H₁₂O₆ + O₂ → CO₂ + H₂O

- **Glycolysis** - *In cytoplasm*
- **Transition Reaction**
- **Citric Acid Cycle** (aka Krebs Cycle)
- **Electron Transport Chain/Chemiosmosis** (Oxidation Phosphorylation)

![[mitochondrion.png]]

### Overview of Four Stages
- **Glycolysis**: "Splits glucose" into pyruvate; ATP, NADH made
- **Transition Reaction**: Moves pyruvate into mitochondria and turn it into acetyl-CoA; CO₂, NADH made
- **Citric Acid Cycle** (aka Krebs Cycle): Systematically dismembers the sugar; CO₂, ATP, NADH, FADH₂ made
- **Electron Transport Chain/Chemiosmosis** (Oxidation Phosphorylation): Uses O₂ to use the NADH and FADH₂ to create a lot of ATP; H₂O made

### Structure of Mitochondria
- Intermembrane space (between the 2 membranes)
- Outer membrane
- Inner membrane
- Matrix (inside of the inner membrane)
- Scale: 0.1 μm

![[mitochondriaStructure.png]]

### Energy Carriers: NADH & FADH₂
These all carry energy that can be used in other places:
- **NADH**: Made from NAD⁺ + H⁺
- **FADH₂**: Made from FAD + 2H⁺

![[energyCarriers.png]]

## Glycolysis! 
#### = splitting glucose *in cytoplasm*

### Process Overview
- Occurs in Cytosol
- Glucose → Pyruvate
- Produces:
	- 2 ATP (Substrate-level phosphorylation)
	- 2 NADH (Electrons carried)

### Energy and Mass Transformation
- **Energy from 1 glucose** transformed to energy in:
	- 2 ATP
	- 2 NADH
	- 2 pyruvate
- **Mass from 1 glucose** transformed to:
	- 2 pyruvate
	- water

![[glycolysis.png]]

### Transition Reaction!
*In mitochondrial matrix*

### Process Components
- **CYTOSOL**: Contains pyruvate
- **MITOCHONDRION**: Contains
	- NAD⁺ → NADH + H⁺
	- CO₂
	- Coenzyme A → Acetyl CoA

### Energy and Mass Transformation
- **Energy from 2 pyruvate** transformed to energy in:
	- 2 NADH
	- 2 Acetyl-CoA
- **Mass from 2 pyruvate + CoA** transformed to:
	- 2 Acetyl-CoA
	- 2 CO₂

![[transitionReaction.png]]

## Citric Acid Cycle
*In mitochondrial matrix*

### Process Description
Ferris Wheel of Sugar Dismemberment:
- Sugars taken apart piece by piece
- Occurs in mitochondrial matrix
- Multiple steps involving various compounds:
	- Acetyl CoA
	- Citrate
	- Isocitrate
	- α-Ketoglutarate
	- Succinyl CoA
	- Succinate
	- Fumarate
	- Malate
	- Oxaloacetate

### Energy and Mass Transformation
- **Energy from 2 Acetyl-CoA** transformed to energy in:
	- 6 NADH
	- 2 FADH₂
	- 2 ATP
- **Mass from 2 Acetyl-CoA + 2 oxaloacetate** transformed to:
	- 2 CoA
	- 2 oxaloacetate
	- 4 CO₂

![[citricAcidCycle.png]]

## Electron Transport Chain and Chemiosmosis!
*Across inner membrane*

### Process Components
1. **Electron transport chain**:
	- Energy from electrons in NADH & FADH₂ used to pump H⁺ into intermembrane space
	- O₂ likes electrons; turns to water
	- Location: Inner mitochondrial membrane

2. **Chemiosmosis**:
	- Energy from the concentration gradient of H⁺ used to make ATP using the enzyme ATP synthase
	- Location: Across inner membrane

### ATP Synthase Function
- As the H⁺ come through, they make the enzyme rotate
- The rotation adds Pi to ADP to make ATP
- Located between:
	- Intermembrane Space
	- Mitochondrial Matrix

![[atpSynthase.png]]

### Energy and Mass Transformation
- **Energy from 10 NADH and 2 FADH₂** transformed to:
	- Energy in H⁺ gradient
	- Transformed to energy in 32-34 ATP
- **Mass from NADH, FADH₂, O₂, ADP, Pi** transformed to:
	- NAD⁺
	- FAD
	- Water
	- ATP

### Case Study Analysis
#### Evidence from Patient
- The girl died within two hours of first vomiting
- Tissues sections from lung, heart, & kidneys show massive cell death
- Staining with cellular dyes indicates that the mitochondria within the affected tissues were damaged
- More detailed analysis of heart cells showed that ATP levels were reduced in the mitochondria
- ATP levels in the cytoplasm of these cells, however, were normal
- Acetyl-CoA molecule levels were normal

#### Metabolite Levels
| Metabolite | Autopsy Finding | Normal Levels |
|------------|----------------|---------------|
| Glucose | 102 μmol | 100 μmol |
| Pyruvate | 23 μmol | 25 μmol |
| NAD⁺ | 6 μmol | 75 μmol |
| NADH | 383 μmol | 50 μmol |

#### Pieces of Evidence Analysis
- Mitochondria were affected: **Probably NOT glycolysis**
- Acetyl-CoA levels normal: **Probably NOT transition reaction**
- NADH levels high: **Probably not citric acid cycle; probably electron transport chain**
- NAD⁺ levels low: **Probably electron transport chain**

#### Conclusion
All the poisons in the Journal affected the electron transport chain, although arsenic affects the other three stages as well.
