### Weight Loss

- Why does breathing in O2 and out CO2 (cellular respiration) cause weight loss?
- Because of the mass difference between CO2 and O2:
  - Breath in the weight of O2 = 16 moles
  - Breathe out the weight of CO2 = 28 moles
  - Net change in weight = 16-28 = -12 moles

#### Example: Christian Bale Weight Loss

- Actor Christian Bale lost 60 pounds for a movie role
- According to cellular respiration, where did most of the dry biomass go?

##### Options:
A. He breathed it out
  - **This is cellular respiration**
B. He pooped it out
  - Your poop mostly has bacteria and food you didn't absorb in the first place
C. He sweated it out
  - Sweat is mostly water and salts, not carbon
D. It left as heat energy
  - Mass cannot disappear
  - Mass cannot turn into heat energy

### Cell Signaling Components

#### Question: What ingredients would a cell need to detect and respond to things going on outside of it?

- **Signal**
  - In the body, mostly chemical signals, including hormones
- **A way to detect or receive that signal**
- **A way to relay that signal to the rest of the cell**

### What a Cell Does When It Receives a Signal

#### Signal Reception Process:
- It uses a **receptor** to detect the signal
  - Receptors are **specific** for one or a few signals
  - Something that binds to a receptor is a **ligand**
- The receptor starts a **signal transduction cascade** inside the cell
- The signaling cascade causes the cell to make some sort of **response**:
  - Activate an enzyme to do a chemical reaction
  - Change shape
  - Expressing genes

![[cellSignaling.png]]

### Possible Effects of Signals on Cells

After the receptor binds to the signal, the receptor starts molecular pathways inside the cell (signal transduction pathways/cascades) that cause various effects.

#### Examples:
- Activating certain enzymes
- Causing release of calcium or other ions
- Gene expression

### Example: Signaling through HER2

1. Signal/ligand
2. Receptor
3. Signal transduction pathways
4. Cell's responses

![[HER2signaling.png]]

#### Question Analysis:
- If the ligand was present but HER2 was not, would the ligand stimulate the cell to divide?
  - **Answer: No**
  - The signaling is done through the receptor

- If the ligand was not present but HER2 was mutated in some way that allowed it still be active, would the cell be stimulated to divide?
  - **Answer: Yes**

### Receptor Location

- Most signals have receptors that are located **within the cell membrane**
  - Parts stick out of the membrane on both sides
- But some signals have receptors located in the **cytoplasm or nucleus**
  - The signal must pass through the membrane to get to them
